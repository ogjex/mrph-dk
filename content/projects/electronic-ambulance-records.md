## The content is used for the description of the project
---

In 2010 multiple Danish Regions decided to digitise their ambulance records. But how? As part of my PhD I was the test and pilot manager of organising and evaluating various versions of how electronic ambulance records worked in real life scenarios in the ambulances.

It gave me tremendeous insight into how real life work was enacted in the ambulances and the intricacies of testing, preparing and evaluating how IoT and mobile devices needed to seamlessly interact to support tasks. 

The learnings of the pilot project paved the way for the 2016 national tender of electronic ambulance records in the whole of Denmark, still in use to this day.
