## 1st job: Technical supporter for Apple and Macintosh servers and desktops

Beginning as a student helper I managed the backup servers (Retrospect) for the Macintosh computers at our department, ranging about 10 users. I had the opportunity to familiarize myself with the basics of Unix commands and server level hardware and software as well as solving technical troubleshooting tasks such as repairing work stations and arranging and scheduling backups on a weekly basis.

From this job began my own interest in Unix-like systems and ensured that I could navigate later on in my Master of Computer Science where I could setup SVN servers, use LaTeX and got into Emacs. I also learned the value of networking and treating everyone around me as equal human beings and listen and solve problems in a calm manner.
