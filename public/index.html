A selection of my projects

The Game For Green Project

Borne by the need for increasing awareness of green behaviour and more responsible power consumption, the Game For Green project is a 2.5 million Euro project that designs, constructs and builds a game engine that supports serious game creation for companies, organisations and municipalities that wish to educate their employees, users and/or citizens for more responsible actions that align with green values. The project was sponsored by the Innovation Fund Denmark and started in May 2023.

My main accomplishments were as initial principal investigator (project manager) who wrote the application and ensured organising and facilitating the vision for the 8 collaborating contributors of the project: Roskilde University, Actee, Provice, Implement Group, HOFOR, Municipalities of Høje Taastrup, Vejle and Vallensbæk.

I have never been solely responsible for handling so many organisational actors with such a large amount of grant money, and it has truly been a learning experience on so many levels.

Case Study

Knowledge Network Assessments

In collaboration with Thy Erhvervsforum I designed, constructed and evaluated an easy-to-use tool for evaluating the health of a knowledge network.

Knowledge Networks are an essential part of sharing experiences and building innovation competences for organisations where select employees participate in monthly to annual meetups with other organisations to discuss and share matters.

Especially for Small to Medium Enterprises reaching out to others and connecting in physical face-to-face networks has shown to provide a huge boost in productivity and growth.

With the knowledge network assessment tool members and facilitators alike can assess the health of a network in order to continuously improve and grow the network with the aim of producing value.

Case Study

UX design process evaluation

For a minor chemical SME I performed a UX evaluation of an existing product by teaching the IT development team how to perform usability evaluations. Through the usability evaluation I proposed a “design system” as guidelines for further design in order to avoid additional problematic UX design decisions.

Case Study

Regional Electronic Ambulance Records

In 2010 multiple Danish Regions decided to digitise their ambulance records. But how? As part of my PhD I was the test and pilot manager of organising and evaluating various versions of how electronic ambulance records worked in real life scenarios in the ambulances.

It gave me tremendeous insight into how real life work was enacted in the ambulances and the intricacies of testing, preparing and evaluating how IoT and mobile devices needed to seamlessly interact to support tasks.

The learnings of the pilot project paved the way for the 2016 national tender of electronic ambulance records in the whole of Denmark, still in use to this day.
